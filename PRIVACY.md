# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-09**

## Introduction

This privacy policy applies to **The Holy Quran** app for Android devices, developed by **Mohamed Ouachmi** (the "Service Provider").

## Information Collection and Use

**The Application does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.**

The app is designed to work entirely offline. No user data is transmitted to external servers, and no accounts are required.

The app does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track user behavior
- Use analytics services
- Display advertisements
- Require login or registration

## Third-Party Services

The app uses two third-party services for specific features:

1. **everyayah.com** — For downloading Quran audio recitations. Only audio files are downloaded; no personal data is sent.

2. **ipapi.co** — For detecting approximate location (country/city) to calculate Qibla direction and prayer times. Only the user's IP address is sent for this purpose. No personal data is stored.

## Offline Functionality

All Quran text, translations, tafsir, and adhkar are bundled with the app and work 100% offline.

## Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages. It does not collect any information from children or anyone else.

## Your Rights

Since the app does not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete.

## Security

The app does not store any user data, so there is no risk of data breach.
